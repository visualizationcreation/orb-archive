The Museum now has a saved, authored arrangement of the 15 published entries (including two separately saved Musée d’Orsay editions) observed on this date. These are editorial navigation relationships, not scientific similarity scores or new contributed editions.

Each has an introduction, member reasons and a stable ID. Original editions, creators, libraries, media, catalog IDs and publication storage are unchanged.

`orb-connect-pass.mjs` owns the authored groups and edges and the pure neighborhood model. `orb-connect-snapshot.mjs` retains only public catalog references observed during this pass, allowing the map to work when the community feed is unavailable. Live listings override the snapshot. Newly published works not in this pass appear under New arrivals rather than disappearing or receiving fabricated relationships.

`orb-browsing.mjs` stores visible route occurrences separately from the saved graph. Clicking any visible orb immediately unfolds up to eight smaller children in a local cluster around that exact point. Child spheres shrink by a factor of 0.64 per layer; click targets remain at least 44 pixels. New children animate out from their parent, respecting reduced motion. Existing positions and other branches remain intact. Call Orbs adds another batch only to the selected branch; it never silently expands an unrelated point.

A saved orb may appear under multiple parents, using a unique route key and the same original publication ID. Shared occurrences have dotted cross-links. A route never repeats one of its own ancestors, and repeated clicks do not duplicate children. Four layers are available per branch: reaching the fourth pauses only that branch, while shallower siblings can still unfold. Refocus branch starts a fresh four-layer window with a route back through Wider view and browser history. There is no overall journey-depth limit. Leaf nodes retain their original published ORB links and do not invent deeper content. Clicking a point also pans the map toward its local cluster.

The lower catalog, original editions and shareable `?map=<stable-id>` links remain available. Museum browsing follows the saved authored graph; it does not generate new topics or call an AI service. App Call Orbs uses its existing discovery service, but now uses the same visitor-controlled three/four-layer refocus boundary. Hybrid formation and Orb Calibrate remain independent.

This is a completed one-time pass authored by the primary assistant. It is not a scheduled daily curator and does not call an AI model when a visitor opens the Museum.
