Call Orbs adds up to eight unseen neighbors per call; earlier layers and cross-links remain visible. Selecting an orb shows its details without replacing the map. At three layers the UI suggests choosing a branch. At four layers further growth pauses until the visitor explicitly refocuses. Refocus branch creates a fresh view; Wider view and browser history restore the saved arrangement. The map scrolls in both directions to preserve readable labels. Rearrange changes only positions. Repeated relationships reuse the same logical orb, and finite collections report when all connected orbs are visible.

The lower catalog, original editions and shareable `?map=<stable-id>` links remain available. Museum browsing follows the saved authored graph; it does not generate new topics or call an AI service. App Call Orbs uses its existing discovery service, but now uses the same visitor-controlled three/four-layer refocus boundary. Hybrid formation and Orb Calibrate remain independent.

This is a completed one-time pass authored by the primary assistant. It is not a scheduled daily curator and does not call an AI model when a visitor opens the Museum.
