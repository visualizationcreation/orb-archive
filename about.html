About ORB

A subject becomes a place to explore.

ORB is a way to explore a subject through connected points. Explore connected ideas, change direction, and follow the questions that interest you.

Using the archive

Search titles, topics, and keywords, or filter by the formats available. Explore opens the interactive ORB. Listen opens the available audio; Watch opens its video or film study. Versions & sources shows the edition, origin details, and source repository.

You can read and explore at your own pace, with or without audio. Update dates describe the cataloged ORB edition; sources within each ORB carry its evidence and context.

The six directions

↑ UpBroader context: the whole that contains this point.
↓ DownFiner detail: a part, mechanism, or piece of evidence.
← Left / Right →Related facets at a similar level of detail.
↶ BackwardOrigins, premises, or earlier stages of a thread.
Forward ↷Consequences, implications, or later stages.

A guided route supplies a listening or reading order. The conceptual compass remains a separate way to navigate.

Spiral Courses

Choose your spiral: follow the ideas, or step into the experience. Spiral Courses guide you through an ORB in a connected sequence, while the interactive ORB remains open for your own exploration.

ORB Learn — Spiral Audio Course

Follow a curious guide through the subject. Hear how the ideas connect through clear explanations, concrete examples and discoveries that build from one point to the next.

ORB Feel & Experience — Guided Audio Journey

Settle in and imagine yourself within the subject. Sensory detail and a gently guided perspective make space for relaxation, reflection and experiencing the connections.

Both modes draw on the ORB's information and sources. Explore opens the interactive ORB; Listen opens the published recording. Available courses and durations vary by ORB and edition.

Create your own ORB

The simplest sharing route is a text file. Your AI writes the points and connections; ORB Share provides the reader.

  1. Explore a topic. Copy the ORB prompt into your AI chat, then type /orb your topic. Follow questions and check sources.
  2. Create the file. In ORB Share, copy the authoring brief into that same chat. Ask for your exploration as an ORB File 1 named your-topic.orb.txt. No HTML or plugin installation is needed.
  3. Preview and save. Open the AI’s file in ORB Share. If it gives you text instead, copy the JSON inside its code block, choose Paste text → Check & open, then Save ORB file. If there are errors, copy them back to your AI and ask it to fix the file.
  4. Submit for review. Choose Prepare submission, check your title and public creator credit, then continue to GitHub. Sign in or create a GitHub account, attach the saved .orb.txt to the issue description, and submit. Keep the issue link as your receipt. Previewing does not upload the file or publish it.
