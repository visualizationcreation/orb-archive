About ORB

ORB is a way to explore a subject through connected points. Open a reading, change direction, and follow the questions that interest you.

Using the archive

Search titles, topics, and keywords, or filter by the formats available. Explore opens the interactive ORB. Listen opens its narrated reader; Watch opens its video or film study. Versions & sources shows the edition, origin details, and source repository.

Readings remain available independently of narration. Update dates describe the cataloged ORB edition; sources within each ORB carry its evidence and context.

The six directions

↑ UpBroader context: the whole that contains this point.
↓ DownFiner detail: a part, mechanism, or piece of evidence.
← Left / Right →Related facets at a similar level of detail.
↶ BackwardOrigins, premises, or earlier stages of a thread.
Forward ↷Consequences, implications, or later stages.

A guided route supplies a listening or reading order. The conceptual compass remains a separate way to navigate.

Reading and journey modes

Each ORB has its own available formats. The archive shows recorded narration and video only when they are published.

Active Journey · Education Journey

A connected, factual explanation of the ORB's points, written for reading or narration.

Rest Journey · Creative Rest Orb Journey

A gently guided, imagined first-person experience of the same information, with poetic language that retains the facts and their qualifications.

These are script styles. A completed script does not mean audio is available. Existing editions retain their recorded labels; newer journey modes appear in the archive when explicitly cataloged.
