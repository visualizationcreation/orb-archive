HTML Orb Museum

A museum of connected worlds.

A place to keep what people have explored, made, and learned — and to find a path into it.

Each orb is a finished work

Open an orb to explore its readings, follow real sources, and use the recordings or films its creator included. The museum is browsed through saved orbs and their connections. No prompt, AI account, or token key is needed.

Visit the museum →

The library belongs with the orb

A complete edition keeps the materials that support it: source links, images, documents, and any uploaded audio. The catalog points to these collections and recordings so visitors can reach beyond a generated answer.

Earlier works remain available in their original form, with source records and edition history. Their recordings stay paired with the text they were made for.

Made by a community

New ORBs can be published directly from the ORB app, up to three per day in one browser. You can sign an orb with your name, studio, or chosen credit, or choose anonymous credit. Add a personal note and describe how you would like the orb expressed in its About this orb point. You can also share public profile links and introduce your ideas, projects, designs, or products in From the contributor. These are separate from the orb’s sources. Tools and source authors retain their own attribution.

Related subjects can be connected without removing their different perspectives or earlier editions.
