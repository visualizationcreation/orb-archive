Orbiversity

A collection you can explore.

Orbiversity holds published ORBs: readings, sources, and paths into other subjects.

Follow what is already here

Choose an orb in the browsing window or catalog. Open its saved edition, move among its readings, and follow its source links. Browsing the collection does not require a prompt, an AI account, or a token key.

Browse the collection →

Read, listen, and watch

Each edition lists what it includes. Some offer recorded learning courses, guided audio journeys, or companion films. Readings may be written by people or created with AI assistance; the edition keeps its authorship and source information.

Linked images, recordings, and external sources may need an internet connection.

Grow the collection together

Contribute a finished ORB for owner review. New contributions are checked before joining the collection. Related editions keep their creator credits and original links.
