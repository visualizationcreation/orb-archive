One ORB. Five ways to explore.

Browse a finished exploration, or choose how to create your own.

ORB turns a subject into connected ideas, illustrated readings and optional listening. These five routes explain where it runs, who supplies the AI, and what is ready today.

01

With ChatGPT

Private preview

Turn your conversation into an ORB.

The ChatGPT plugin direction connects ORB to a supported ChatGPT account. Ask about a topic, explore the result, and continue the conversation to create new directions.

Who pays / the benefit
Text creation uses your available ChatGPT allowance, without a separate text-generation API account. Generated audio and other media may use separately funded services.
What to consider
The embedded reader has limited space. In the separate website reader, follow-up prompts currently need to be taken back to ChatGPT. Features also depend on the host application.
Where it stands
A private connection has been tested. A public directory listing is not yet available; setup depends on your ChatGPT account and workspace.

02

With OpenRouter

Planned integration

Use the ORB website with your own AI credits.

The planned connection would let you fund a personal OpenRouter account and create ORBs using supported models inside the ORB website.

Who pays / the benefit
You would pay OpenRouter for your model requests. This keeps personal AI spending under your control. OpenRouter credits are separate from a ChatGPT subscription and do not pay ORB a membership fee.
What to consider
You would need another account and a credit balance. Writing quality, speed and access to current information depend on the supported model and research tools.
Where it stands
Personal account connection is still in development. Buying credits today does not automatically connect them to ORB.

03

With a local LLM

Experimental setup

Create with a model running on your computer.

This route pairs a local ORB installation with Ollama and a supported model, such as the prepared Gemma setup. It is for people comfortable installing and configuring software.

Who pays / the benefit
Your hardware and electricity supply the text generation, avoiding per-request cloud-model charges. Optional online services can still have costs.
What to consider
Speed and quality depend on your computer and model. The current local trial does not include live web research, cloud speech, online publishing or media lookup.
Where it stands
The adapter and launch scripts are prepared; real-machine performance is still being checked. There is no tested public ORB installer yet. Ollama alone does not install ORB.

04

Explore saved ORBs (HTML)

Available now

Open a finished ORB and follow your curiosity.

Choose from the published collection. Move between prepared points, read the included passages, explore images and sources, and enjoy recordings where the edition includes them.

Who pays / the benefit
You do not need an AI account or token balance to read and navigate these finished editions. No new model request is needed for that exploration.
What to consider
You explore the material already included. New answers, images or OrbCast episodes require a creation route. Downloaded HTML may still need the internet for external images, audio and links.
Where it stands
The published collection is ready to browse. Original content may be AI-assisted; each edition retains its own sources and credits. Complete offline download packages are still being evaluated.

05

ORB Membership

Planned service

One ORB account, with AI service included.

The membership direction would let you sign into ORB and create without choosing an external provider or installing a local model. ORB would operate and fund the AI service.

Who pays / the benefit
Members would pay ORB. ORB would pay the model providers and the hosting, storage and media services included in the plan.
What to consider
A recurring charge and a defined service allowance would apply. The aim is comfortable exploration with clear inclusions; unlimited generation has not been promised.
Where it stands
Membership accounts, payments and pricing are not available yet. The existing app preview is not a paid membership product.
