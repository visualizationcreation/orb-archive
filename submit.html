Add to Orbiversity

Share a finished ORB with the collection.

Submit for owner review

The current submission route uses GitHub. Share a published link or attach an ORB file, with a title, short description, and creator credit. A GitHub account is required.

Your submission and attachments on GitHub are public. Include only material you have permission to share.

Prepare a submission ↗

Submitting does not publish your ORB. The owner reviews each contribution before it joins the vault.

What to include

  • A complete ORB with readable points and working links.
  • Creator credit, source links, and permission to host the submitted edition.
  • Any images or recordings the edition needs, with their credits.

After review

Accepted editions keep their original credit and provenance. Related ORBs can be grouped without erasing the different perspectives or sources they contain.
