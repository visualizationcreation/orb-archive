An idea becomes a place to explore

Change direction.
Keep the thread.

ORB is a prompting method and a set of tools for navigating ideas with AI. A topic gives you an anchor. Directions let you change scale, find neighbors, and follow a line of thought.

Up and down change the level of detail. Left and right visit related ideas. Forward and backward follow consequences, origins, or the next and previous stages of a process.

The fuller system also turns directions into actions on a piece of work and offers a rhythm mode for exploring patterns across time. An anchor reading helps you see how far the route has moved from your original subject.
