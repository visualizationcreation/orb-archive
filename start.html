Start with an orb.

No setup. Choose a subject that catches your attention.

    Select a sphere in the browsing window, or follow a title in the catalog. Topic and format filters help narrow the collection.

  2. Follow its readings and sources

    Use the edition’s own orb controls and links. Included recordings and films are marked in the catalog.

  3. Return and try another path

    Use your browser’s Back button to return to the vault. Versions & sources lists the earlier editions and their provenance.
